# MERU Standing Desk — Assembly Guide

The KRIYA MERU Standing Desk is a height-adjustable desk with a crank mechanism, featuring a laminated MDF top and steel frame. Dimensions: 140 cm (W) x 70 cm (D) x 75–120 cm (H). Package weight: 28 kg.

## Components

- A — MERU Standing Desk tabletop (laminated MDF, 140 x 70 cm) x 1
- B — MERU Standing Desk left leg assembly (steel, telescoping) x 1
- C — MERU Standing Desk right leg assembly (steel, telescoping) x 1
- D — MERU Standing Desk lower crossbar (steel, 120 cm) x 1
- E — MERU Standing Desk upper crossbar (steel, 130 cm) x 1
- F — MERU Standing Desk crank mechanism housing x 1
- G — MERU Standing Desk crank handle (detachable) x 1
- H — MERU Standing Desk foot plates (steel, 60 x 8 cm) x 2
- I — MERU Standing Desk cable management tray x 1
- J — Hex bolts (M6 x 20 mm) x 16
- K — Hex bolts (M8 x 25 mm) x 8
- L — Lock washers (M6) x 16
- M — Lock washers (M8) x 8
- N — Wood screws (5 x 16 mm) x 12
- O — Rubber leveling feet x 4
- P — Allen key (5 mm) x 1
- Q — Allen key (6 mm) x 1

## Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver (PH2)
- Adjustable wrench or 10 mm / 13 mm socket wrench
- Level
- Tape measure
- Rubber mallet

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all MERU Standing Desk components and verify every item against the components list above. Lay the MERU Standing Desk tabletop face-down on a clean, padded surface to protect the laminate finish.

2. Attach the two MERU Standing Desk foot plates (H) to the bottom of the left and right leg assemblies (B, C). Align the bolt holes and secure each foot plate using two M8 hex bolts (K) with lock washers (M). Tighten firmly with the wrench.

3. Thread the four rubber leveling feet (O) into the pre-threaded holes at each corner of the MERU Standing Desk foot plates (H). Leave them at mid-height for now.

4. Connect the MERU Standing Desk lower crossbar (D) between the left leg assembly (B) and the right leg assembly (C). Align the bolt holes on the inner faces of the legs and secure with four M6 hex bolts (J) and lock washers (L) — two per side.

5. Attach the MERU Standing Desk upper crossbar (E) between the upper portions of the left and right leg assemblies (B, C). Secure with four M6 hex bolts (J) and lock washers (L) — two per side. The frame should now stand freely.

6. Install the MERU Standing Desk crank mechanism housing (F) onto the left leg assembly (B) at the designated mounting point. Align the gear teeth with the telescoping shaft inside the leg and secure with four M6 hex bolts (J) and lock washers (L).

7. Insert the MERU Standing Desk crank handle (G) into the crank mechanism housing (F). Push until it clicks into the retention slot. Test by rotating the crank clockwise — the MERU Standing Desk legs should extend smoothly. Rotate counter-clockwise to retract.

8. With the MERU Standing Desk frame standing upright, lower the desk to its minimum height using the crank handle. Place the MERU Standing Desk tabletop (A) face-down on the floor. Carefully invert the frame and set it centered on the underside of the tabletop.

9. Align the MERU Standing Desk frame mounting holes with the pre-drilled pilot holes on the underside of the tabletop (A). Secure the frame to the tabletop using twelve wood screws (N) — six along the upper crossbar and three on each side bracket.

10. Flip the MERU Standing Desk assembly right-side up. This step requires two people — one at each end. Lift from the foot plates, not the tabletop, to avoid stressing the screw joints.

11. Attach the MERU Standing Desk cable management tray (I) to the underside of the tabletop (A) along the rear edge. Secure with the remaining M6 hex bolts (J) and lock washers (L) using the pre-drilled holes.

12. Use a level on the MERU Standing Desk tabletop and adjust the four rubber leveling feet (O) until the desk is stable and level. Test the crank mechanism through the full height range (75 cm to 120 cm) to confirm smooth operation.

## Safety Warnings

- The MERU Standing Desk package weighs 28 kg. Use two people to lift and move the MERU Standing Desk to avoid injury.
- Do not exceed 80 kg load on the MERU Standing Desk tabletop. This includes monitors, equipment, and leaning force.
- Keep hands and fingers away from the MERU Standing Desk telescoping leg joints while operating the crank mechanism. Pinch risk exists at the sliding joint.
- Ensure the MERU Standing Desk is on a flat, stable floor before use. The telescoping mechanism can bind or fail on uneven surfaces.
- Do not force the MERU Standing Desk crank handle if resistance is felt. Stop, check for obstructions or misalignment, and correct before continuing.
- Tighten all MERU Standing Desk hex bolts fully before use. Re-check bolt tightness after the first week of use and every six months thereafter.
- Do not sit or stand on the MERU Standing Desk tabletop. The MDF top is rated for distributed loads only, not concentrated point loads.
- Keep the MERU Standing Desk crank handle folded or detached when not adjusting height to prevent accidental bumps.
