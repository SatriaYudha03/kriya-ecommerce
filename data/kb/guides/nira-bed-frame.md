# NIRA Bed Frame — Assembly Guide

## Components

- A — Left side rail (1x) — pine wood, 200 x 25 cm
- B — Right side rail (1x) — pine wood, 200 x 25 cm
- C — Headboard panel (1x) — pine wood, 160 x 90 cm
- D — Footboard panel (1x) — pine wood, 160 x 35 cm
- E — Headboard leg (2x) — pine wood, 90 cm
- F — Footboard leg (2x) — pine wood, 35 cm
- G — Center support beam (1x) — pine wood, 200 cm
- H — Center support leg (2x) — pine wood, 25 cm
- I — Slat (14x) — pine wood, 78 x 8 cm
- J — Slat holder strip (2x) — fabric, 200 cm
- K — Side rail bracket (4x) — steel
- L — Bolt M6 x 60 mm (12x)
- M — Barrel nut (12x)
- N — Confirmat screw 50 mm (16x)
- O — Wooden dowel (12x)
- P — Washer (12x)
- Q — Allen key (1x, included)

## Tools Required

- Allen key (included) or electric drill with hex bit
- Phillips-head screwdriver
- Rubber mallet
- Tape measure
- Spirit level

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all NIRA Bed Frame components onto a clean, flat surface. Confirm every part against the components list before beginning assembly of the NIRA Bed Frame.

2. Attach the NIRA Bed Frame headboard legs (E) to the headboard panel (C). Insert wooden dowels (O) into the pre-drilled holes on each leg, apply light pressure, then secure with confirmat screws (N) through the back of each leg into the headboard panel.

3. Attach the NIRA Bed Frame footboard legs (F) to the footboard panel (D) using the same dowel-and-confirmat method as step 2.

4. Install a side rail bracket (K) at each end of both NIRA Bed Frame side rails (A and B). Align the bracket with the pre-drilled holes and fasten with bolts (L) and barrel nuts (M). Use a washer (P) under each bolt head.

5. Connect the NIRA Bed Frame left side rail (A) to the headboard assembly. Hook the side rail bracket into the slot on the inner face of the headboard leg (E). Insert bolts (L) through the leg into the barrel nuts (M) on the bracket. Tighten with the Allen key.

6. Connect the opposite end of the NIRA Bed Frame left side rail (A) to the footboard assembly using the same bracket-and-bolt method as step 5.

7. Repeat steps 5 and 6 for the NIRA Bed Frame right side rail (B), connecting it to the headboard and footboard on the other side.

8. Place the NIRA Bed Frame center support beam (G) along the middle of the frame, resting on the inner ledges of the headboard panel (C) and footboard panel (D). Secure each end with confirmat screws (N).

9. Attach the two NIRA Bed Frame center support legs (H) to the underside of the center support beam (G) at the pre-marked positions (approximately one-third and two-thirds along the beam). Fasten each leg with confirmat screws (N).

10. Lay the NIRA Bed Frame slat holder strips (J) across the side rails and center beam, one strip on each half of the frame. The strips keep slats evenly spaced.

11. Place the 14 NIRA Bed Frame slats (I) across the frame, distributing seven slats on each side of the center beam. Each slat should rest on the side rail ledge and the center support beam. Ensure even spacing of approximately 5 cm between slats.

12. Verify the completed NIRA Bed Frame is level using a spirit level across the side rails and headboard. Adjust the center support legs if the frame wobbles. Tighten all bolts to final torque.

## Safety Warnings

- The NIRA Bed Frame weighs 38 kg. Two adults are required for assembly to handle the side rails and headboard safely.
- Do not exceed the maximum mattress and user load of 250 kg on the NIRA Bed Frame.
- Check all NIRA Bed Frame bolt connections after the first week of use and retighten if necessary — pine wood may settle slightly.
- Do not allow children to jump on the NIRA Bed Frame; this can crack the pine slats or loosen the bracket connections.
- Place the NIRA Bed Frame on a flat, even floor. Uneven surfaces cause joint stress and may lead to structural failure over time.
- Keep all small hardware (bolts, barrel nuts, screws) away from children during NIRA Bed Frame assembly.
- Do not drag the assembled NIRA Bed Frame across the floor. Lift and carry with a helper to prevent leg damage and floor scratching.
