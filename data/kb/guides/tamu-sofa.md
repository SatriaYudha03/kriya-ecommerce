# TAMU Sofa — Assembly Guide

## Components

- A — Left armrest frame (1x) — pine wood, 85 x 80 cm
- B — Right armrest frame (1x) — pine wood, 85 x 80 cm
- C — Seat frame base (1x) — pine wood, 160 x 85 cm
- D — Backrest frame (1x) — pine wood, 160 x 45 cm
- E — Seat cushion (3x) — high-density foam with polyester fabric cover
- F — Back cushion (3x) — fibre-fill with polyester fabric cover
- G — Support leg (4x) — pine wood, 12 cm
- H — Center support leg (1x) — pine wood, 12 cm
- I — Webbing strap (6x) — elastic, pre-stapled to seat frame
- J — Armrest bolt M8 x 80 mm (8x)
- K — Barrel nut (8x)
- L — Backrest bracket (4x) — steel
- M — Backrest bolt M6 x 50 mm (8x)
- N — Washer (16x)
- O — Leg mounting screw M8 x 25 mm (10x)
- P — Allen key 5 mm (1x, included)
- Q — Allen key 4 mm (1x, included)

## Tools Required

- Allen keys (included) or electric drill with hex bit set
- Phillips-head screwdriver
- Tape measure
- Scissors (for unpackaging only)

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all TAMU Sofa components on a clean, flat surface. Verify every part against the components list before beginning TAMU Sofa assembly. Remove all protective wrapping from the frames but leave cushion covers on.

2. Place the TAMU Sofa seat frame base (C) upside down on a soft surface (use the packaging cardboard to protect the floor and frame).

3. Screw the four TAMU Sofa support legs (G) into the pre-drilled corner holes on the underside of the seat frame base (C). Insert a washer (N) under each leg head, then tighten with leg mounting screws (O) using the Allen key.

4. Screw the TAMU Sofa center support leg (H) into the pre-drilled hole at the center of the seat frame base underside. Use two leg mounting screws (O) with washers (N).

5. Carefully flip the TAMU Sofa seat frame base (C) right-side up. Confirm it stands level and does not rock. The elastic webbing straps (I) should be visible across the top of the seat frame.

6. Attach the TAMU Sofa left armrest frame (A) to the seat frame base (C). Align the two pre-drilled bolt holes on the armrest with the corresponding holes on the left side of the seat frame. Insert armrest bolts (J) through the armrest into the barrel nuts (K) seated in the frame. Place a washer (N) under each bolt head. Tighten with the 5 mm Allen key until snug — do not overtighten.

7. Attach the TAMU Sofa right armrest frame (B) to the opposite side of the seat frame base (C) using the same method as step 6.

8. Position the four TAMU Sofa backrest brackets (L) on the rear edge of the seat frame base (C). Align each bracket with the pre-drilled holes and loosely thread the backrest bolts (M) with washers (N) — do not fully tighten yet.

9. Lift the TAMU Sofa backrest frame (D) and align its bottom edge holes with the upper holes of the four backrest brackets (L). Insert backrest bolts (M) with washers (N) through the brackets into the backrest frame. Hand-tighten all eight bracket bolts.

10. Adjust the TAMU Sofa backrest frame (D) angle until the backrest sits at a comfortable recline (the bracket slots allow slight angle adjustment). Once satisfied, fully tighten all backrest bolts (M) with the 4 mm Allen key.

11. Place the three TAMU Sofa seat cushions (E) onto the seat frame base (C). They should fit snugly between the two armrests with no gaps.

12. Place the three TAMU Sofa back cushions (F) upright against the backrest frame (D). Arrange them evenly across the width of the sofa.

13. Perform a final check of the assembled TAMU Sofa: sit on each seat position to verify stability, confirm all legs contact the floor, check that armrests do not wobble, and ensure the backrest is firmly secured.

## Safety Warnings

- The TAMU Sofa weighs 35 kg. Two adults are recommended for assembly, particularly when attaching the backrest frame.
- Do not exceed a total seating load of 270 kg (90 kg per seat) on the TAMU Sofa.
- Do not stand on the TAMU Sofa seat cushions or armrests. The pine wood frame is designed for seated loads only.
- Check all TAMU Sofa bolt connections after the first two weeks of use and retighten if any have loosened.
- Keep all small hardware (bolts, barrel nuts, washers) away from children during TAMU Sofa assembly.
- The TAMU Sofa legs may scratch hard flooring. Apply felt pads (not included) to the bottom of each leg before placing on hardwood, tile, or laminate floors.
- Do not drag the assembled TAMU Sofa across the floor. Lift with a helper to reposition.
- Keep the TAMU Sofa polyester fabric cushion covers away from open flames and direct heat sources.
