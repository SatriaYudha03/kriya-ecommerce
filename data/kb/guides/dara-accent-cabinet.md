# DARA Accent Cabinet — Assembly Guide

The KRIYA DARA Accent Cabinet is a display cabinet with tempered glass doors and a mahogany frame. Dimensions: 90 cm (W) x 40 cm (D) x 85 cm (H). Package weight: 28 kg.

## Components

- A — DARA Accent Cabinet top panel (mahogany, 90 x 40 cm) x 1
- B — DARA Accent Cabinet side panels (mahogany, 40 x 83 cm) x 2
- C — DARA Accent Cabinet bottom panel (mahogany, 90 x 40 cm) x 1
- D — DARA Accent Cabinet back panel (HDF, 86 x 81 cm) x 1
- E — DARA Accent Cabinet center shelf (mahogany, 86 x 38 cm) x 1
- F — DARA Accent Cabinet tempered glass door panels (4 mm, 42 x 70 cm) x 2
- G — DARA Accent Cabinet door frames (mahogany, pre-assembled with glass channel) x 2
- H — DARA Accent Cabinet concealed hinges (110°) x 4
- I — DARA Accent Cabinet magnetic door catches x 2
- J — DARA Accent Cabinet door knobs (brass-finished) x 2
- K — DARA Accent Cabinet shelf support pins (metal) x 4
- L — Cam lock bolts x 16
- M — Cam lock nuts x 16
- N — Wooden dowels (8 mm) x 12
- O — Pan-head screws (4 x 16 mm) x 20
- P — Back panel nails (15 mm) x 14
- Q — Hinge mounting screws (3.5 x 16 mm) x 16
- R — Door knob mounting screws (M4 x 25 mm) x 4
- S — Adjustable feet x 4
- T — Felt pads x 4

## Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver (PH2)
- Rubber mallet or soft-faced hammer
- Small hammer (for back panel nails)
- Level
- Tape measure

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all DARA Accent Cabinet components and verify every item against the components list above. Lay parts on a clean, soft surface. Handle the DARA Accent Cabinet tempered glass door panels (F) with particular care — keep them in their protective sleeves until step 9.

2. Insert wooden dowels (N) into the pre-drilled holes on the top edge of the DARA Accent Cabinet bottom panel (C). Press dowels in by hand or tap lightly with the rubber mallet until they seat fully.

3. Attach the DARA Accent Cabinet left side panel (B) to the bottom panel (C). Align the dowels with the corresponding holes in the side panel, then secure with cam lock bolts (L) and cam lock nuts (M). Tighten the cam nuts clockwise with a Phillips-head screwdriver.

4. Insert four shelf support pins (K) into the pre-drilled pin holes on the inner faces of the DARA Accent Cabinet left side panel (B), at the desired shelf height. Two pins go in the left side panel now; the other two will go in the right side panel in step 6.

5. Place the DARA Accent Cabinet center shelf (E) onto the two installed shelf support pins (K) on the left side panel. The shelf rests on the pins and is not permanently fixed, allowing height adjustment later.

6. Attach the DARA Accent Cabinet right side panel (B) to the bottom panel (C) using dowels and cam locks, following the same method as step 3. Before fully tightening, insert the remaining two shelf support pins (K) into the right side panel at the matching height and rest the DARA Accent Cabinet center shelf (E) on all four pins. Tighten all cam nuts.

7. Attach the DARA Accent Cabinet top panel (A) to both side panels (B). Insert dowels (N) into the top edges of the side panels, align the top panel, and secure with cam lock bolts (L) and cam lock nuts (M). Verify the cabinet is square by measuring diagonals — they should match within 2 mm.

8. Stand the DARA Accent Cabinet upright. Attach the back panel (D) using back panel nails (P). Nail into the rear edges of the top, bottom, and side panels at approximately 12 cm intervals. The back panel provides critical structural rigidity to the DARA Accent Cabinet.

9. Slide each DARA Accent Cabinet tempered glass door panel (F) into the channel groove of its corresponding door frame (G). The glass should seat fully into the channel with no exposed edges. If the glass panels were shipped pre-installed in the frames, skip this sub-step.

10. Mount the four concealed hinges (H) onto the DARA Accent Cabinet door frames (G) — two hinges per door. Attach the hinge arm to the pre-drilled 35 mm cup bore on the inner edge of each door frame using hinge mounting screws (Q).

11. Attach the DARA Accent Cabinet door assemblies to the cabinet body. Mount the hinge base plates to the inner faces of the side panels (B) using hinge mounting screws (Q) in the pre-drilled pilot holes. Clip each hinge arm onto its base plate until it clicks.

12. Install the two DARA Accent Cabinet magnetic door catches (I) on the inside face of the top panel (A), centered above each door. Secure with pan-head screws (O). Attach the corresponding metal strike plates to the inside top edge of each door frame using pan-head screws (O).

13. Attach the DARA Accent Cabinet brass-finished door knobs (J) to the front face of each door frame (G). Insert door knob mounting screws (R) from the inside of the frame and tighten.

14. Adjust the DARA Accent Cabinet concealed hinges (H) using the three-way adjustment screws on each hinge. Adjust until both doors are flush, evenly gapped, and open and close smoothly without rubbing.

15. Screw the four adjustable feet (S) into the bottom corners of the DARA Accent Cabinet. Use a level on the top panel and adjust feet until the cabinet is stable and level. Apply felt pads (T) to protect flooring.

## Safety Warnings

- The DARA Accent Cabinet package weighs 28 kg. Use two people to lift and unpack the DARA Accent Cabinet to avoid injury.
- The DARA Accent Cabinet contains tempered glass panels. Although tempered glass is safer than standard glass, it can still shatter into small pieces if struck with force. Handle with care during assembly.
- Wear gloves and safety glasses when handling the DARA Accent Cabinet tempered glass door panels (F) to protect against edge contact and unexpected breakage.
- Anchor the DARA Accent Cabinet to the wall using a tip-over restraint (not included) to prevent tipping, especially in households with children or in earthquake-prone areas.
- Do not exceed 10 kg distributed load per DARA Accent Cabinet shelf.
- Do not exceed 15 kg total load on the DARA Accent Cabinet top panel.
- Do not allow children to swing on or hang from the DARA Accent Cabinet glass doors. The hinges are rated for the door weight only.
- Keep all DARA Accent Cabinet hardware (screws, nails, pins) away from small children to prevent choking hazards.
- If a DARA Accent Cabinet tempered glass panel is cracked or chipped, do not install it. Contact KRIYA customer support for a replacement.
