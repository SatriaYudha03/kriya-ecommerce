# LOKA Dresser — Assembly Guide

The KRIYA LOKA Dresser is a six-drawer mahogany dresser with brass-finished handles. Dimensions: 100 cm (W) x 45 cm (D) x 80 cm (H). Package weight: 30 kg.

## Components

- A — LOKA Dresser top panel (mahogany, 100 x 45 cm) x 1
- B — LOKA Dresser side panels (mahogany, 45 x 80 cm) x 2
- C — LOKA Dresser bottom panel (mahogany, 100 x 45 cm) x 1
- D — LOKA Dresser back panel (HDF, 96 x 76 cm) x 1
- E — LOKA Dresser horizontal dividers (mahogany, 96 x 43 cm) x 2
- F — LOKA Dresser vertical divider (mahogany, 43 x 24 cm) x 3
- G — LOKA Dresser drawer box (assembled, 46 x 40 x 12 cm) x 6
- H — LOKA Dresser drawer front face (mahogany, 47 x 13 cm) x 6
- I — LOKA Dresser brass-finished handles x 6
- J — LOKA Dresser drawer slides (left/right pair) x 6
- K — Cam lock bolts x 24
- L — Cam lock nuts x 24
- M — Wooden dowels (8 mm) x 20
- N — Pan-head screws (4 x 30 mm) x 28
- O — Back panel nails (15 mm) x 16
- P — Handle mounting screws (M4 x 25 mm) x 12
- Q — Adjustable feet x 4
- R — Felt pads x 4

## Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver (PH2)
- Rubber mallet or soft-faced hammer
- Level
- Tape measure
- Small hammer (for back panel nails)

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all LOKA Dresser components and verify every item against the components list above. Lay parts on a clean, soft surface to avoid scratching the mahogany finish.

2. Insert four wooden dowels (M) into the pre-drilled holes on the inner face of the LOKA Dresser bottom panel (C). Press them in firmly by hand or tap gently with the rubber mallet.

3. Attach the LOKA Dresser left side panel (B) to the bottom panel (C). Align the dowels with the corresponding holes in the side panel, then secure using three cam lock bolts (K) and cam lock nuts (L). Tighten the cam nuts with a Phillips-head screwdriver by turning them clockwise.

4. Install the first LOKA Dresser horizontal divider (E) into the left side panel (B). Insert dowels (M) into the divider's edge, align with the pre-drilled holes in the side panel, and lock with cam lock bolts and nuts.

5. Install the second LOKA Dresser horizontal divider (E) above the first, following the same method as step 4. This creates three rows of drawer openings for the LOKA Dresser.

6. Attach the LOKA Dresser right side panel (B) onto the exposed dowels and cam bolts of the bottom panel and both horizontal dividers. Tighten all cam lock nuts securely.

7. Install the three LOKA Dresser vertical dividers (F), one per row, into the center slots of each horizontal section. Secure each vertical divider with two dowels (M) top and bottom.

8. Place the LOKA Dresser top panel (A) onto the assembly. Align dowels and cam bolts, then lock all cam nuts. Confirm the dresser frame is square by measuring diagonals — they should be equal within 2 mm.

9. Stand the LOKA Dresser upright. Attach the back panel (D) using the 15 mm back panel nails (O). Nail into the rear edges of the side panels, top, bottom, and dividers at approximately 15 cm intervals.

10. Install the six pairs of LOKA Dresser drawer slides (J). Mount the cabinet-side rail of each slide using pan-head screws (N), aligning with the pre-drilled pilot holes inside each drawer compartment.

11. Attach the drawer-side rail of each LOKA Dresser drawer slide (J) to the corresponding drawer box (G) using pan-head screws (N). Ensure the rail is flush with the front edge of the drawer box.

12. Mount a brass-finished handle (I) onto each LOKA Dresser drawer front face (H) using two handle mounting screws (P) per handle. Insert the screws from the back side of the face panel and tighten.

13. Attach each LOKA Dresser drawer front face (H) to its drawer box (G) using four pan-head screws (N) from inside the drawer box. Ensure even spacing between faces when all drawers are inserted.

14. Slide all six LOKA Dresser drawer boxes into their corresponding compartments. Test each drawer for smooth operation — slides should extend and retract without binding.

15. Screw the four adjustable feet (Q) into the bottom corners of the LOKA Dresser. Use a level across the top panel and adjust feet until the dresser is perfectly level. Apply felt pads (R) under each foot to protect flooring.

## Safety Warnings

- The LOKA Dresser weighs 30 kg when packed. Use two people to lift and unpack the LOKA Dresser to avoid back injury.
- Anchor the LOKA Dresser to the wall using a tip-over restraint (not included) to prevent tipping, especially in households with children.
- Do not allow children to climb or hang on the LOKA Dresser drawers. Open drawers shift the center of gravity forward and can cause the dresser to tip.
- Do not exceed 5 kg load per LOKA Dresser drawer to prevent slide failure.
- Do not exceed 15 kg total load on the LOKA Dresser top panel.
- Place the LOKA Dresser on a flat, stable surface. Uneven floors may cause instability.
- Wear gloves when handling the LOKA Dresser mahogany panels to avoid splinters along unfinished edges.
- Keep all LOKA Dresser hardware (screws, nails, cam locks) away from small children to prevent choking hazards.
