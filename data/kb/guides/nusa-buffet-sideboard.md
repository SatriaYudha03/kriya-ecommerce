# NUSA Buffet Sideboard — Assembly Guide

## Components

- A — Left side panel (1x) — mahogany, 45 x 80 cm
- B — Right side panel (1x) — mahogany, 45 x 80 cm
- C — Top panel (1x) — mahogany, 160 x 45 cm
- D — Bottom panel (1x) — mahogany, 160 x 45 cm
- E — Back panel (1x) — mahogany veneer MDF, 158 x 78 cm
- F — Interior vertical divider (1x) — mahogany, 44 x 58 cm
- G — Interior shelf (2x) — mahogany, 78 x 43 cm
- H — Sliding door (2x) — mahogany, 80 x 60 cm
- I — Sliding door upper track (1x) — steel, 158 cm
- J — Sliding door lower track (1x) — steel, 158 cm
- K — Steel leg (4x) — matte black steel, 15 cm
- L — Leg mounting plate (4x) — steel
- M — Cam lock bolt (14x)
- N — Cam lock disc (14x)
- O — Wooden dowel (16x)
- P — Confirmat screw 50 mm (10x)
- Q — Shelf support pin (8x)
- R — Back panel nail (12x)
- S — Leg mounting screw M6 x 16 mm (16x)

## Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver or electric drill with Phillips bit
- Allen key (included)
- Hammer
- Rubber mallet
- Tape measure
- Spirit level

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all NUSA Buffet Sideboard components on a clean, flat surface. Verify every part against the components list before starting the NUSA Buffet Sideboard assembly.

2. Lay the NUSA Buffet Sideboard bottom panel (D) flat with the inner face up. Insert wooden dowels (O) into the pre-drilled holes along the left edge, right edge, and center divider position.

3. Insert cam lock bolts (M) into the pre-drilled holes on the NUSA Buffet Sideboard left side panel (A), right side panel (B), and interior vertical divider (F). Ensure the arrow on each bolt points outward toward the panel edge.

4. Attach the NUSA Buffet Sideboard left side panel (A) to the bottom panel (D). Align dowels and cam lock bolts with their corresponding holes. Press firmly, then tighten each cam lock disc (N) clockwise using a Phillips-head screwdriver.

5. Attach the NUSA Buffet Sideboard interior vertical divider (F) to the bottom panel (D) at the center position, using the same dowel-and-cam-lock method as step 4.

6. Attach the NUSA Buffet Sideboard right side panel (B) to the bottom panel (D), again using dowels and cam lock connections.

7. Insert shelf support pins (Q) into the desired height positions on the NUSA Buffet Sideboard side panels (A, B) and divider (F). Place the two interior shelves (G) onto the pins — one shelf in each compartment.

8. Attach the NUSA Buffet Sideboard top panel (C) onto the assembly. Align dowels and cam lock bolts from the side panels and divider into the pre-drilled holes on the top panel underside. Tighten all cam lock discs (N).

9. Turn the NUSA Buffet Sideboard assembly face-down. Position the back panel (E) against the rear edges and secure with back panel nails (R), spacing nails approximately 15 cm apart along all edges.

10. With the NUSA Buffet Sideboard still face-down, attach the four leg mounting plates (L) to the underside of the bottom panel at the four corner positions. Fasten each plate with four leg mounting screws (S).

11. Screw each NUSA Buffet Sideboard steel leg (K) into its mounting plate (L) by threading the leg post into the plate socket. Hand-tighten, then firm up with an Allen key.

12. Carefully turn the NUSA Buffet Sideboard upright with a helper. Confirm it stands level using a spirit level; adjust leg height if the legs have adjustable feet.

13. Install the NUSA Buffet Sideboard sliding door upper track (I) into the groove on the underside of the top panel. Press or tap into place with a rubber mallet. Install the lower track (J) into the groove on the top face of the bottom panel.

14. Insert the NUSA Buffet Sideboard sliding doors (H) by tilting each door into the upper track first, then lowering the bottom edge into the lower track. The rear door goes in first, then the front door. Test that both doors slide freely along the full width.

15. Perform a final inspection of the assembled NUSA Buffet Sideboard: slide both doors fully left and right, confirm shelves are level, verify the unit does not rock, and check that all cam locks and screws are tight.

## Safety Warnings

- The NUSA Buffet Sideboard weighs 38 kg. Two adults are required to flip and position the unit during assembly.
- Do not exceed 20 kg load per shelf on the NUSA Buffet Sideboard to prevent shelf bowing.
- Do not exceed 30 kg total load on the NUSA Buffet Sideboard top panel.
- Keep fingers clear of the NUSA Buffet Sideboard sliding door tracks when inserting or removing doors — pinch hazard.
- The NUSA Buffet Sideboard steel legs have a combined load rating of 120 kg. Do not sit or stand on the sideboard.
- Keep all small hardware (screws, nails, cam locks) away from children during NUSA Buffet Sideboard assembly.
- Place the NUSA Buffet Sideboard on a flat, level surface. Uneven floors can stress the mahogany joints and cause the sliding doors to bind.
- Avoid dragging the assembled NUSA Buffet Sideboard. The steel legs may scratch flooring and loosen from their mounting plates.
