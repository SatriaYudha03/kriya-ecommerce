# GALA Wardrobe — Assembly Guide

## Components

- A — Left side panel (1x) — laminated MDF, 55 x 200 cm
- B — Right side panel (1x) — laminated MDF, 55 x 200 cm
- C — Top panel (1x) — laminated MDF, 120 x 55 cm
- D — Bottom panel (1x) — laminated MDF, 120 x 55 cm
- E — Back panel (1x) — laminated MDF, 120 x 200 cm
- F — Interior shelf (3x) — laminated MDF, 58 x 53 cm
- G — Hanging rail (1x) — steel tube, 58 cm
- H — Hanging rail bracket (2x) — steel
- I — Left door (1x) — laminated MDF, 59 x 198 cm
- J — Right door (1x) — laminated MDF, 59 x 198 cm
- K — Door hinge (6x) — steel
- L — Door handle (2x) — steel
- M — Cam lock bolt (16x)
- N — Cam lock disc (16x)
- O — Wooden dowel (20x)
- P — Confirmat screw 50 mm (12x)
- Q — Back panel nail (16x)
- R — Shelf support pin (12x)
- S — Adjustable foot (4x)

## Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver or electric drill with Phillips bit
- Hammer
- Tape measure
- Spirit level
- Allen key (included)
- Rubber mallet

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all GALA Wardrobe components on a clean, flat surface. Verify every part against the components list above before starting assembly.

2. Lay the GALA Wardrobe left side panel (A) flat on the floor with the inner face up. Insert wooden dowels (O) into the pre-drilled holes along the top edge, bottom edge, and shelf-support column.

3. Insert cam lock bolts (M) into the pre-drilled holes on the GALA Wardrobe top panel (C), bottom panel (D), and shelves (F). Turn each bolt so the arrow faces outward toward the panel edge.

4. Attach the GALA Wardrobe bottom panel (D) to the left side panel (A). Align the dowels and cam lock bolts with the corresponding holes. Press firmly, then tighten each cam lock disc (N) clockwise with a Phillips-head screwdriver.

5. Insert shelf support pins (R) into the desired height positions on the GALA Wardrobe left side panel (A) and right side panel (B). Place the three interior shelves (F) onto the pins on the left side panel for now; they will be secured once the right panel is attached.

6. Install the hanging rail brackets (H) on the inner faces of the GALA Wardrobe left side panel (A) and right side panel (B) using confirmat screws (P). Position brackets at the pre-marked holes near the top of each panel. Slide the hanging rail (G) into the left bracket.

7. Attach the GALA Wardrobe top panel (C) to the left side panel (A) using dowels and cam lock connections, following the same method as step 4.

8. Raise the GALA Wardrobe assembly upright with a helper. Slide the right side panel (B) onto the exposed dowels and cam lock bolts of the top panel (C), bottom panel (D), and shelves (F). Slot the hanging rail (G) into the right bracket (H). Tighten all cam lock discs (N) on the right side.

9. Secure the GALA Wardrobe hanging rail brackets (H) with confirmat screws (P) if not yet fully tightened from step 6.

10. Position the GALA Wardrobe back panel (E) against the rear edges of the assembled frame. Nail it in place using back panel nails (Q), spacing nails approximately 15 cm apart along all four edges.

11. Screw the adjustable feet (S) into the four corner holes on the underside of the GALA Wardrobe bottom panel (D). Adjust until the wardrobe stands level — use a spirit level to confirm.

12. Attach three door hinges (K) to the GALA Wardrobe left door (I): position hinges at 10 cm from the top, 10 cm from the bottom, and centered. Repeat for the right door (J). Use confirmat screws (P) for each hinge.

13. Mount each GALA Wardrobe door onto the side panels by screwing the hinge plates into the pre-drilled holes on the inner edges of panels A and B. Adjust hinge screws until doors open and close smoothly with even gaps.

14. Attach the door handles (L) to the GALA Wardrobe doors (I and J) using the bolts provided. Position each handle at the pre-drilled hole approximately 100 cm from the floor.

15. Perform a final check of the assembled GALA Wardrobe: open and close both doors, confirm all shelves are level, verify the hanging rail is secure, and ensure the unit does not rock.

## Safety Warnings

- The GALA Wardrobe weighs 55 kg. Two adults are required for assembly and positioning to prevent injury.
- Anchor the GALA Wardrobe to the wall using an anti-tip strap or L-bracket (not included) to prevent tipping, especially in households with children.
- Do not exceed the maximum shelf load of 15 kg per shelf on the GALA Wardrobe.
- Do not exceed the maximum hanging rail load of 25 kg on the GALA Wardrobe.
- Keep all small hardware (screws, dowels, cam locks) away from children during GALA Wardrobe assembly.
- Assemble the GALA Wardrobe on a soft surface or protective sheet to avoid scratching floors or panel finishes.
- Do not use power tools at excessive torque settings on the GALA Wardrobe laminated MDF panels — overtightening may strip the pre-drilled holes.
