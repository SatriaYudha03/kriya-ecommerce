# KOPI Kitchen Island — Assembly Guide

The KRIYA KOPI Kitchen Island features a butcher-block pine wood top with a steel shelf below. Dimensions: 120 cm (W) x 60 cm (D) x 90 cm (H). Package weight: 28 kg.

## Components

- A — KOPI Kitchen Island butcher-block top (pine wood, 120 x 60 x 4 cm) x 1
- B — KOPI Kitchen Island steel legs (square tube, 85 cm) x 4
- C — KOPI Kitchen Island upper side frame rails (steel, 52 cm) x 2
- D — KOPI Kitchen Island upper front/back frame rails (steel, 112 cm) x 2
- E — KOPI Kitchen Island lower steel shelf (perforated steel, 110 x 50 cm) x 1
- F — KOPI Kitchen Island lower side support rails (steel, 52 cm) x 2
- G — KOPI Kitchen Island lower front/back support rails (steel, 112 cm) x 2
- H — KOPI Kitchen Island towel bar (steel, 40 cm) x 1
- I — Hex bolts (M6 x 15 mm) x 24
- J — Hex bolts (M8 x 20 mm) x 8
- K — Lock nuts (M6) x 24
- L — Lock nuts (M8) x 8
- M — Flat washers (M6) x 24
- N — Flat washers (M8) x 8
- O — Wood screws (5 x 20 mm) x 8
- P — Leveling feet with rubber pads x 4
- Q — Allen key (5 mm) x 1
- R — Allen key (6 mm) x 1
- S — Mineral oil packet for butcher-block conditioning x 1

## Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver (PH2)
- Adjustable wrench or 10 mm / 13 mm socket wrench
- Level
- Tape measure
- Rubber mallet
- Clean lint-free cloth (for mineral oil application)

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all KOPI Kitchen Island components and verify every item against the components list above. Lay parts on a clean, flat surface. Inspect the KOPI Kitchen Island butcher-block top (A) for shipping damage.

2. Attach the two KOPI Kitchen Island upper side frame rails (C) to two of the four steel legs (B). Insert M6 hex bolts (I) through the pre-drilled holes on the rails into the threaded inserts on the legs. Place flat washers (M) under each bolt head and tighten with the Allen key. This creates the left side frame.

3. Repeat step 2 for the remaining two KOPI Kitchen Island steel legs (B) and the second upper side frame rail (C) to create the right side frame.

4. Connect the left and right KOPI Kitchen Island side frames using the two upper front/back frame rails (D). Insert M6 hex bolts (I) with flat washers (M) through the rail ends into the leg threaded inserts. Hand-tighten only at this stage to allow adjustment.

5. Install the two KOPI Kitchen Island lower side support rails (F) between the legs on each side, approximately 20 cm from the floor. Secure with M6 hex bolts (I), flat washers (M), and lock nuts (K).

6. Install the two KOPI Kitchen Island lower front/back support rails (G) between the front legs and between the rear legs at the same 20 cm height. Secure with M6 hex bolts (I), flat washers (M), and lock nuts (K). The lower rail frame must be level and square.

7. Place the KOPI Kitchen Island lower steel shelf (E) onto the four lower support rails (F, G). Align the shelf mounting holes with the holes on the rails and secure with M8 hex bolts (J), flat washers (N), and lock nuts (L).

8. Square the entire KOPI Kitchen Island steel frame by measuring diagonals across the top — both measurements should match within 3 mm. Once square, fully tighten all M6 hex bolts on the upper frame rails from step 4.

9. Attach the KOPI Kitchen Island towel bar (H) to the right side of the frame. Secure to the outer face of the right side upper frame rail (C) using two M6 hex bolts (I) and lock nuts (K).

10. Place the KOPI Kitchen Island butcher-block top (A) onto the upper frame. Center it so that equal overhang exists on all sides. Secure the top to the upper frame rails using eight wood screws (O) driven upward through the pre-drilled holes in the rails into the underside of the butcher-block top.

11. Thread the four leveling feet (P) into the bottom of each KOPI Kitchen Island steel leg (B). Use a level on the butcher-block top and adjust feet until the KOPI Kitchen Island is stable and does not rock.

12. Apply the included mineral oil (S) to the KOPI Kitchen Island butcher-block top (A) using a clean lint-free cloth. Spread evenly across the entire surface, let it soak for 20 minutes, and wipe off excess. This protects the pine wood from moisture and stains.

## Safety Warnings

- The KOPI Kitchen Island package weighs 28 kg. Use two people to unpack and assemble the KOPI Kitchen Island to avoid injury.
- The KOPI Kitchen Island butcher-block top is food-safe only when treated with the included mineral oil. Do not use chemical cleaners directly on the KOPI Kitchen Island wood surface.
- Do not exceed 50 kg distributed load on the KOPI Kitchen Island butcher-block top.
- Do not exceed 25 kg distributed load on the KOPI Kitchen Island lower steel shelf.
- The KOPI Kitchen Island is freestanding furniture and is not designed to be anchored to walls or floors. Ensure it is placed on a level surface before loading.
- Do not use the KOPI Kitchen Island as a step stool or standing platform. The steel frame is designed for storage loads, not human weight.
- Re-apply mineral oil to the KOPI Kitchen Island butcher-block top monthly or whenever the wood appears dry, to maintain its protective seal.
- Keep all KOPI Kitchen Island hardware (bolts, nuts, washers) away from small children to prevent choking hazards.
- Wear gloves when handling the KOPI Kitchen Island steel shelf edges — stamped steel edges may be sharp.
