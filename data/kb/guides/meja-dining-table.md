# MEJA Dining Table — Assembly Guide

## Components

- A — Tabletop (1x) — teak wood, 150 x 85 cm
- B — Table leg (4x) — teak wood, 72 cm
- C — Long apron rail (2x) — teak wood, 130 x 8 cm
- D — Short apron rail (2x) — teak wood, 65 x 8 cm
- E — Corner brace bracket (4x) — steel
- F — Leg mounting bolt M8 x 70 mm (8x)
- G — Barrel nut (8x)
- H — Tabletop connector bolt M6 x 20 mm (8x)
- I — Wooden dowel (16x)
- J — Washer (16x)
- K — Tabletop alignment pin (4x) — steel
- L — Felt pad (4x) — adhesive-backed
- M — Allen key 5 mm (1x, included)
- N — Allen key 4 mm (1x, included)

## Tools Required

- Allen keys (included) or electric drill with hex bit set
- Rubber mallet
- Tape measure
- Spirit level
- Soft cloth (for wiping teak surfaces)

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all MEJA Dining Table components on a clean, flat surface. Verify every part against the components list before starting MEJA Dining Table assembly. Handle the teak tabletop (A) with care to avoid surface scratches.

2. Place the MEJA Dining Table tabletop (A) face-down on a soft, protective surface (use the packaging cardboard or a blanket).

3. Assemble the MEJA Dining Table apron frame: insert wooden dowels (I) into the pre-drilled holes at each end of the two long apron rails (C) and two short apron rails (D). Connect the rails into a rectangle by pressing dowelled ends together. Use a rubber mallet to seat the joints fully.

4. Attach the first MEJA Dining Table leg (B) to one corner of the apron frame. Insert two leg mounting bolts (F) with washers (J) through the pre-drilled holes in the apron rails into the barrel nuts (G) embedded in the leg. Tighten with the 5 mm Allen key until firm.

5. Repeat step 4 for the remaining three MEJA Dining Table legs (B), attaching each to its respective corner of the apron frame.

6. Install the four MEJA Dining Table corner brace brackets (E) on the inside of each corner where the apron rails meet. Align each bracket with the pre-drilled screw holes and fasten with tabletop connector bolts (H) and washers (J). Leave the upper bolt holes empty for now — these will attach to the tabletop.

7. Position the assembled MEJA Dining Table leg-and-apron frame onto the underside of the tabletop (A). Align the tabletop alignment pins (K) on the apron rails with the corresponding holes on the tabletop underside. Press the frame down so the pins seat fully.

8. Secure the MEJA Dining Table tabletop to the frame by inserting tabletop connector bolts (H) through the upper holes of the corner brace brackets (E) into the pre-drilled holes on the tabletop underside. Tighten with the 4 mm Allen key.

9. Carefully flip the MEJA Dining Table right-side up with a helper. Lift from the apron rails, not the tabletop edges.

10. Check that the MEJA Dining Table stands level using a spirit level placed on the tabletop surface. If the table rocks, check that all four legs are fully tightened and the floor surface is even.

11. Apply the four adhesive-backed felt pads (L) to the bottom of each MEJA Dining Table leg to protect flooring.

12. Wipe down the MEJA Dining Table teak tabletop with a soft, dry cloth to remove any dust or fingerprints from assembly.

## Safety Warnings

- The MEJA Dining Table weighs 32 kg. Two adults are required to flip the table during assembly to prevent injury and damage to the teak surface.
- Do not exceed a total load of 80 kg on the MEJA Dining Table tabletop (distributed evenly). Do not sit or stand on the table surface.
- Retighten all MEJA Dining Table leg bolts after the first month of use. Teak wood may expand or contract slightly with humidity changes, which can loosen connections.
- Place the MEJA Dining Table on a flat, level floor. Uneven surfaces create rocking that stresses the apron joints over time.
- Do not drag the assembled MEJA Dining Table. Lift with a helper to reposition and avoid damaging the leg joints and floor.
- Keep all small hardware (bolts, barrel nuts, dowels) away from children during MEJA Dining Table assembly.
- Avoid placing the MEJA Dining Table in direct prolonged sunlight. UV exposure can fade and dry out the teak wood finish.
- Use coasters and placemats on the MEJA Dining Table teak surface. Hot cookware and wet glasses can leave permanent marks on untreated teak.
