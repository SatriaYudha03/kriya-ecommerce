# WIRA Bookshelf — Assembly Guide

The KRIYA WIRA Bookshelf is a five-tier open bookshelf made from pine wood. Dimensions: 80 cm (W) x 30 cm (D) x 180 cm (H). Package weight: 25 kg.

## Components

- A — WIRA Bookshelf top panel (pine wood, 80 x 30 cm) x 1
- B — WIRA Bookshelf side panels (pine wood, 30 x 178 cm) x 2
- C — WIRA Bookshelf bottom panel (pine wood, 80 x 30 cm) x 1
- D — WIRA Bookshelf fixed shelves (pine wood, 76 x 28 cm) x 3
- E — WIRA Bookshelf back panel (HDF, 76 x 176 cm) x 1
- F — WIRA Bookshelf rear crossbrace (pine wood, 74 x 8 cm) x 2
- G — Cam lock bolts x 20
- H — Cam lock nuts x 20
- I — Wooden dowels (8 mm) x 16
- J — Pan-head screws (4 x 30 mm) x 12
- K — Back panel nails (15 mm) x 18
- L — Wall anchor bracket x 1
- M — Wall anchor screws (5 x 50 mm) x 2
- N — Adjustable feet x 4
- O — Felt pads x 4

## Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver (PH2)
- Rubber mallet or soft-faced hammer
- Small hammer (for back panel nails)
- Level
- Tape measure
- Power drill with 6 mm masonry or wood bit (for wall anchoring)

## Assembly Steps

1. Unpack all WIRA Bookshelf components and verify every item against the components list above. Lay all WIRA Bookshelf panels flat on a clean surface. Sort hardware into groups for easy access during assembly.

2. Lay the WIRA Bookshelf left side panel (B) on the floor with its inner face up. Insert wooden dowels (I) into the pre-drilled dowel holes at each shelf position — the bottom edge plus three fixed shelf locations evenly spaced along the panel.

3. Attach the WIRA Bookshelf bottom panel (C) to the left side panel (B). Align the dowels with the corresponding holes, then secure with cam lock bolts (G) and cam lock nuts (H). Tighten the cam nuts clockwise with a Phillips-head screwdriver.

4. Install the first WIRA Bookshelf fixed shelf (D) into the left side panel (B) at the lowest shelf position. Insert dowels (I) into the shelf edge, align with the side panel holes, and lock with cam lock bolts (G) and cam lock nuts (H).

5. Install the second WIRA Bookshelf fixed shelf (D) at the middle shelf position, and the third WIRA Bookshelf fixed shelf (D) at the upper shelf position, following the same method as step 4 for each shelf.

6. Attach the WIRA Bookshelf right side panel (B) to the exposed dowels and cam bolts on the bottom panel (C) and all three fixed shelves (D). Work from bottom to top, aligning each shelf before tightening. Tighten all cam lock nuts securely.

7. Attach the WIRA Bookshelf top panel (A) to both side panels (B). Insert dowels (I) into the top edges of the side panels, place the top panel, and secure with cam lock bolts (G) and cam lock nuts (H). Verify the WIRA Bookshelf frame is square by measuring diagonals — they should match within 3 mm.

8. Attach the two WIRA Bookshelf rear crossbraces (F) to the back of the frame using pan-head screws (J). Mount one crossbrace diagonally from the upper-left corner area to the mid-right area, and the second from the upper-right to the mid-left, forming an X pattern. These crossbraces provide lateral rigidity to the tall WIRA Bookshelf frame.

9. Stand the WIRA Bookshelf upright. This requires two people due to the 180 cm height. One person should stabilize the base while the other tilts the bookshelf up.

10. Attach the WIRA Bookshelf back panel (E) using back panel nails (K). Nail into the rear edges of the side panels, top panel, bottom panel, and each fixed shelf at approximately 12 cm intervals. Start from the top and work downward, keeping the back panel aligned with the frame edges.

11. Screw the four adjustable feet (N) into the pre-threaded holes at the bottom corners of the WIRA Bookshelf. Use a level across the top panel and adjust feet until the bookshelf is perfectly level and does not wobble.

12. Secure the WIRA Bookshelf to the wall using the wall anchor bracket (L). Attach the bracket to the rear top edge of the WIRA Bookshelf using two pan-head screws (J). Drill into the wall stud or use appropriate wall plugs, then fasten the bracket to the wall using the wall anchor screws (M). This step is mandatory for safety.

13. Apply felt pads (O) under each adjustable foot of the WIRA Bookshelf to protect flooring from scratches.

## Safety Warnings

- The WIRA Bookshelf package weighs 25 kg. Use two people to unpack and assemble the WIRA Bookshelf to avoid injury.
- Wall anchoring is mandatory for the WIRA Bookshelf. At 180 cm tall, the WIRA Bookshelf presents a serious tipping hazard if not secured to a wall stud or masonry. Do not skip step 12.
- Do not exceed 15 kg distributed load per WIRA Bookshelf tier. Overloading any shelf may cause sagging or structural failure.
- Do not exceed 10 kg load on the WIRA Bookshelf top panel.
- Load the WIRA Bookshelf from the bottom tier upward. Place heavier items on lower shelves to keep the center of gravity low.
- Do not climb on or use the WIRA Bookshelf as a ladder. Use a step stool to reach upper tiers.
- Do not allow children to climb the WIRA Bookshelf. The open-shelf design can invite climbing, which creates a tipping hazard.
- Place the WIRA Bookshelf on a flat, stable floor. Carpet or uneven flooring may affect stability — adjust leveling feet accordingly.
- Keep all WIRA Bookshelf hardware (screws, nails, dowels) away from small children to prevent choking hazards.
- Inspect the WIRA Bookshelf wall anchor every six months to ensure it remains tight and secure.
