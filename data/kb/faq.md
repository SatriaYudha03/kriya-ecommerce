# KRIYA — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### Q1: What is KRIYA?
KRIYA is an Indonesian flat-pack furniture brand. We design modern, affordable furniture that you assemble yourself at home.

### Q2: Where is KRIYA based?
KRIYA is based in Indonesia. We ship to all provinces across the country.

### Q3: Are KRIYA products made in Indonesia?
Yes. All KRIYA furniture is designed and manufactured in Indonesia using locally sourced and imported materials.

### Q4: What does "flat-pack" mean?
Flat-pack means the furniture arrives disassembled in a flat box. You assemble it at home using the included hardware and our step-by-step guide.

### Q5: Do I need special tools to assemble KRIYA furniture?
Most products only require a Phillips screwdriver and an Allen key (included). Larger items like bed frames and wardrobes may need a power drill. Check the assembly guide for each product.

## Products

### Q6: What materials does KRIYA use?
We use pine wood, teak wood, mahogany, bamboo, rattan, steel, aluminum, laminated MDF, tempered glass, polyester fabric, and synthetic leather depending on the product.

### Q7: How do I choose the right color variant?
Each product page shows available color options with images. Popular options include natural, white, black, walnut, oak, grey, and cream.

### Q8: Can I order replacement parts?
Yes. Contact our customer service with your order number and the part you need. Replacement parts are available for up to two years after purchase.

### Q9: Are assembly guides available online?
Yes. Products marked with "Assembly guide available" have a detailed step-by-step guide accessible from the product page.

### Q10: What is the weight capacity of KRIYA shelves?
Weight capacity varies by product. Check the product description for specific load limits. As a general guide, wall shelves support up to 15 kg per shelf and freestanding bookcases support up to 10 kg per tier.

## Orders

### Q11: How do I place an order?
Browse the catalog, add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, enter your shipping address, and complete payment through Midtrans.

### Q12: What payment methods are accepted?
We accept bank transfer, credit card, debit card, and e-wallet payments through Midtrans.

### Q13: Can I modify my order after placing it?
Orders cannot be modified once payment is confirmed. Contact customer service immediately if you need to make changes.

### Q14: How do I check my order status?
Log in to your account and visit the Orders page. You can also ask our chatbot for a status update.

### Q15: What do the order statuses mean?
- **Pending payment**: Order placed, waiting for payment.
- **Paid**: Payment confirmed, preparing your order.
- **Processing**: Your order is being packed.
- **Shipped**: Your order is on the way.
- **Completed**: Delivered successfully.
- **Cancelled**: Order was cancelled.
- **Expired**: Payment window expired.

## Shipping

### Q16: How long does shipping take?
Java and Bali: 3–7 business days. Other provinces: 7–14 business days. Times may vary during peak seasons.

### Q17: How much does shipping cost?
Flat rate shipping: Rp 150,000 for Java and Bali, Rp 300,000 for other provinces.

### Q18: Do you ship internationally?
Currently we only ship within Indonesia.

## Account

### Q19: Do I need an account to browse products?
No. You can browse the full catalog without an account. An account is required to add items to your cart and place orders.

### Q20: How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot password" on the login page. Enter your email and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.
