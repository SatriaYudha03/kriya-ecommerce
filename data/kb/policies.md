# KRIYA — Store Policies

## Shipping Policy

### Coverage
KRIYA ships to all provinces in Indonesia. International shipping is not available at this time.

### Shipping Rates
We use flat-rate shipping:
- **Java and Bali**: Rp 150,000 per order
- **Other provinces**: Rp 300,000 per order

Shipping cost is calculated per order, not per item.

### Delivery Times
- **Java and Bali**: 3–7 business days after payment confirmation
- **Other provinces**: 7–14 business days after payment confirmation

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding national holidays. Delivery times are estimates and may vary during peak seasons or due to carrier delays.

### Tracking
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can also check your order status on the Orders page or through our chatbot.

### Delivery Conditions
- Items are delivered to your door (ground floor). Stair carry or elevator delivery is not included.
- Please inspect the package upon delivery. Report any visible damage to the carrier and contact us within 24 hours.
- Flat-pack items may arrive in multiple boxes. Verify all boxes are received before signing.

## Return Policy

### Eligibility
You may return a product within **14 days** of delivery if:
- The product is defective or damaged upon arrival.
- The product received does not match your order.
- The product is unused, unassembled, and in its original packaging.

### Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- Products that have been assembled (even partially).
- Products with signs of use, stains, or customer-caused damage.
- Mattress toppers and fabric items that have been unpacked from their sealed bag.

### Return Process
1. Contact customer service within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the issue.
2. Our team will review your request within 2 business days.
3. If approved, we will arrange a pickup at no cost to you.
4. Refund is processed within 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

### Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Bank transfer refunds may take an additional 3–5 business days to appear in your account.

## Warranty Policy

### Coverage
All KRIYA furniture comes with a **1-year limited warranty** covering:
- Structural defects in materials (cracked wood, warped MDF, broken welds).
- Hardware failure (drawer slides, hinges, casters) under normal use.

### Exclusions
The warranty does not cover:
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, fading, fabric pilling).
- Damage from misuse, modification, or improper assembly.
- Damage from exposure to water, extreme heat, or direct sunlight beyond normal indoor conditions.
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers.

### Claim Process
1. Contact customer service with your order number, product name, and photos showing the defect.
2. Our team will assess the claim within 5 business days.
3. If approved, we will send a replacement part or offer a replacement product at no charge.
4. In rare cases where repair or replacement is not possible, a full refund will be issued.

## Payment Policy

### Accepted Methods
Payments are processed through Midtrans. We accept:
- Bank transfer (BCA, BNI, Mandiri, BRI, Permata)
- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB)
- Debit card
- E-wallet (GoPay, ShopeePay, OVO, DANA)

### Payment Window
After placing an order, you have **24 hours** to complete payment. Orders not paid within this window will automatically expire.

### Pricing
All prices are listed in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and include applicable taxes. Prices do not include shipping cost, which is added at checkout.

### Invoice
A digital invoice is sent to your email after payment confirmation. You can also download invoices from the Orders page.
